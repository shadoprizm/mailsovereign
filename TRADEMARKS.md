# Sovereign Mail Brand Policy

The AGPL license covers source code; it does not grant permission to present an unofficial fork or
service as an official Sovereign Mail release.

You may accurately refer to Sovereign Mail, link to the canonical repository, describe
compatibility, or state that a project is forked from Sovereign Mail. Do not use the Sovereign Mail
name, logo, or visual identity to imply sponsorship, endorsement, or official release status
without permission.

Unofficial distributions should use a distinct product name and identify their source and
maintainer clearly.
